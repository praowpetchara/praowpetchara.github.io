About Me

Hello, I am praowpetchara. I dwell in the liminal spaces between languages, crafting narratives and transmuting words to bridge cultural divides.

With a deep appreciation for literature and linguistics, my work spans personal essays, contemporary fiction, and translation bridging English and Thai. I believe that every translated sentence is a delicate negotiation, and every original story is an act of vulnerability.

I am open for editorial commissions, translation projects, and literary collaborations.
